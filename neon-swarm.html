Neon Swarm — Privacy Policy

Effective: October 10, 2026

Neon Swarm ("the Game") is published by Nova Celeste Game Studio ("we"). This policy explains what the Game does with your information. The short version: the Game does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

Data we collect

None. The Game does not request the Internet permission, has no analytics, no advertising SDKs, no accounts, and no third-party services. It cannot send data off your device.

Data stored on your device

The Game saves your progress (shards, upgrades, unlocked survivors, best run, settings) in a small local file inside the Game's private app storage. This file never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the Game. You can also erase it at any time from Settings → Reset All Progress inside the Game.

Permissions

  • Vibration — used only for optional haptic feedback. It can be switched off in Settings.

Children

The Game does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If a future update adds any data collection (for example optional ads or cloud saves), this policy will be updated first and the change will be described in the update's release notes.
