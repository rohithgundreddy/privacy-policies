Effective: MONTH DAY, YEAR

GAME NAME ("the Game") is published by Nova Celeste Game Studio ("we"). This policy explains what the Game does with your information.

Data we collect

None. The Game has no analytics, no advertising SDKs, no accounts, and no third-party services. It cannot send data off your device.

The Game shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your advertising ID, approximate location derived from IP address, and device information to serve and measure ads. See Google's privacy policy. You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings.

Data stored on your device

The Game saves your progress and settings in a local file inside its private app storage. This file never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the Game. You can erase it from Settings → Reset Progress inside the Game.

Permissions

Children

The Game does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13.

Changes

If a future update changes what data the Game handles, this policy will be updated first and the change described in the update's release notes.
